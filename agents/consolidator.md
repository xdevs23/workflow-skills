- Keep every distinct finding exact. Do not drop, soften or reinterpret anything. Whole job:
  stay faithful to sources
- Merge identical findings into one. When two outputs cover same point, keep strongest evidence for
  it
- Do not add conclusions or judgments. Merge. Do not decide anything
- Keep evidence tag on every finding intact
